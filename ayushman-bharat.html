Ayushman Bharat

Ayushman Bharat aur PM-JAY ke baare mein simple information — card, eligibility aur treatment se judi basic details.

Ayushman Bharat kya hai?

Ayushman Bharat Government of India ki health protection initiative hai. Iske under different health-related programmes chalaye jaate hain.


PM-JAY iska ek major component hai jo eligible families ko specified hospitalisation treatment ke liye health coverage provide karta hai.

PM-JAY mein kya samjhein?

🏥

Hospital Treatment

Eligible beneficiaries ko covered hospitalisation services ke liye scheme ka benefit mil sakta hai.

🪪

Beneficiary Identification

Benefit lene se pehle beneficiary ki eligibility aur identification verify ki jaati hai.

📋

Eligibility Check

Har person automatically eligible nahi hota. Apni eligibility official system se verify karein.

👨‍⚕️

Empanelled Hospital

Treatment ke liye scheme ke under available empanelled hospital ki information check karna important hai.

⭐ Sabse important

Sirf kisi card ya message dekh kar ye assume mat karein ki treatment automatically covered hai. Hospital, treatment/package aur beneficiary eligibility ko official source se verify karein.

Agar Ayushman ka benefit chahiye to kya karein?

1️⃣

Eligibility Check

Sabse pehle official system ya authorised help point se apni eligibility check karein.

2️⃣

Documents

Jo documents ya identification required ho, unhe ready rakhein.

3️⃣

Hospital Verify Karein

Treatment se pehle hospital aur available scheme coverage ke baare mein confirm karein.

4️⃣

Help Lein

Confusion ho to authorised health worker ya official scheme help channel se information lein.

⚠️ Fraud se bachna

Apni sensitive information protect karein

  • Unknown person ko OTP share na karein.
  • PIN, password ya banking details share na karein.
  • Fake websites ya suspicious messages par blindly trust na karein.
  • Scheme ke naam par paise maangne wale person ko verify karein.

Simple Rule

Pehle eligibility verify → phir hospital verify → phir treatment coverage confirm.


Kisi bhi important health decision ke liye qualified healthcare professional aur official government information ko priority dein.

Important:

Ye page general information ke liye hai. Eligibility aur scheme benefits time ke saath update ho sakte hain, isliye actual benefit lene se pehle official information verify karein.
