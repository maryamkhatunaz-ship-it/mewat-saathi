Simple Flow:
10th → 11th/12th/ITI/Diploma → Entrance/Admission → Course → Skills → Internship/Job/Higher Study

🔬 PCM

Engineering, architecture, computer science, defence, pure sciences, data/AI aur technical fields explore kiye ja sakte hain.

🧬 PCB

Medical, nursing, pharmacy, allied health, biotechnology aur life science related paths explore kiye ja sakte hain.

Law, teaching, civil services preparation, psychology, journalism, languages aur social sciences jaise paths.

⚙️ ITI / Diploma

Technical skills, apprenticeship, employment aur further education ke pathways.

🎓 Graduation ke Baad

Job, competitive exams, professional courses, master's aur specialisation options.

💻 Computer/AI Interest

Programming → projects → portfolio → internship/job → advanced specialisation ka practical route.
