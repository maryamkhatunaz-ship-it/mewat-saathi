Child Health

Bachchon ki health, daily care aur important warning signs ke baare mein simple information.

Bachchon ki health kyun important hai?

Bachchon ka body aur mind abhi develop ho raha hota hai. Isliye unki proper food, clean water, vaccination, rest aur regular health care par dhyan dena important hai.

Basic Child Care

🥗

Healthy Food

Bachche ko age ke according healthy aur nutritious food dene par dhyan dein.

💧

Paani

Bachche ko enough fluids dein, especially jab weather bahut hot ho ya bachcha sick ho.

💉

Vaccination

Bachche ki vaccination schedule ke according vaccines lagwana important hai.

😴

Proper Rest

Bachche ko age ke according enough sleep aur rest milna chahiye.

Agar Bachcha Bimaar Ho

🌡️

Temperature Check

Agar bachcha hot feel kare ya fever ke signs ho, temperature check karein.

🥤

Fluids

Bachcha pee sakta hai to fluids dete rahein, taaki paani ki kami na ho.

👀

Behaviour Dekhein

Bachcha normal active hai ya unusually sleepy/weak, is par dhyan dein.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor ki Advice

Agar symptoms serious hain ya bachcha continuously worse ho raha hai, doctor se contact karein.

⚠️ Important Warning Signs

In signs ko ignore na karein

  • Bachcha properly kha ya pee nahi pa raha
  • Saans lene mein dikkat ya breathing bahut fast hona
  • Bachcha bahut zyada sleepy ya respond na kare
  • Fits/convulsions aana
  • Sab kuch vomit kar dena
  • High fever ke saath condition worse hona
  • Bachcha normal se bahut kam active ho jana

Parents ke liye simple rule

Agar bachcha normal se bahut alag behave kar raha hai, kha-pi nahi raha, breathing mein problem hai ya bahut sleepy ho gaya hai, to wait na karein. Medical help lena better hai.

Kya avoid karein?

💊

⏳

Danger signs dikhein to medical help lene mein unnecessary delay na karein.
