Dental Health

Teeth, gums aur mouth ko healthy rakhne ke simple aur practical basics.

Dental Health kyun important hai?

Healthy teeth aur gums sirf smile ke liye nahi, balki eating, speaking aur overall comfort ke liye bhi important hain.


Bahut si oral health problems early stage mein prevent ya treat ki ja sakti hain.

🪥 Daily Teeth Care

🪥

Din mein 2 baar Brush

WHO fluoride-containing toothpaste ke saath twice-daily toothbrushing recommend karta hai.

🧴

Fluoride Toothpaste

Fluoride toothpaste tooth decay ko prevent karne mein help karta hai.

🍬

Sugar ka Dhyan

Free sugars ki high aur frequent intake tooth decay ka risk badha sakti hai.

🧼

Oral Hygiene

Teeth aur gums ko regularly clean rakhna plaque build-up ko control karne mein help karta hai.

😬 Common Dental Problems

🦷

Tooth Decay

Tooth decay se pain, sensitivity aur untreated cases mein infection ho sakta hai.

🩸

Bleeding Gums

Bleeding ya swollen gums gum disease ka sign ho sakte hain aur ignore nahi karna chahiye.

😮

Bad Breath

Persistent bad breath oral hygiene ya kisi dental problem se related ho sakta hai.

⚡

Tooth Pain

Repeated ya severe tooth pain ko sirf temporary home remedy se ignore nahi karna chahiye.

⭐ Simple Rule

Brush regularly, fluoride toothpaste use karein, sugary foods/drinks ko limit karein aur dental problem persist kare to qualified dentist ya healthcare professional se advice lein.

🩸 Gums ki care

Healthy gums normally clean aur comfortable hone chahiye. Swelling, repeated bleeding, pain ya bad breath jaise symptoms ko continuously ignore na karein.


Gum problems early stage mein identify karna aur appropriate dental care lena important hai.

🍭 Sugar aur Teeth

Mouth mein plaque food aur drinks ke free sugars ko acids mein convert kar sakta hai. Ye process time ke saath tooth enamel ko damage kar sakta hai.


Isliye sugary foods aur drinks ko frequently lene ki habit ko reduce karna oral health ke liye useful hai.

👨‍⚕️ Dentist ko kab dikhayein?

🦷

Continuous Pain

Tooth pain baar-baar ho raha ho ya persist kar raha ho to dental professional se check karayein.

🩸

Repeated Bleeding

Gums repeatedly bleed ya swollen ho rahe hain to evaluation karana useful hai.

😣

Eating mein Problem

Tooth ya mouth problem ki wajah se eating difficult ho rahi ho to professional advice lein.

🚨

Severe Swelling

Face ya mouth mein significant swelling ke saath severe symptoms ho to urgent medical/dental help lein.

⚠️ Important

Dental problem ko unnecessarily delay na karein

  • Severe ya persistent pain ko ignore na karein.
  • Swelling, infection ya repeated bleeding ko seriously lein.
  • Tobacco/chewing tobacco oral health ko harm kar sakta hai.
  • Online information ko professional dental examination ka replacement na samjhein.
