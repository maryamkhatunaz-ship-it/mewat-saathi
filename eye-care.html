Eye Care

Eyes ko healthy rakhne, vision problems ko samajhne aur timely eye care lene ki basic information.

👁️ Eye Care kyun important hai?

Vision daily life, education, work aur normal activities ke liye bahut important hai.


Kai eye conditions ko early detection aur timely treatment se manage kiya ja sakta hai. Isliye vision mein noticeable change ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

🔎 Common Eye Problems

👓

Refractive Error

Myopia, hyperopia aur astigmatism jaise refractive errors blurred vision ka cause ho sakte hain.

💧

Dry Eye

Dry eye mein eyes uncomfortable, dry ya irritated feel ho sakti hain.

🔴

Conjunctivitis

Redness, irritation aur discharge jaise symptoms conjunctivitis mein ho sakte hain.

🌫️

Cataract

Cataract eye ke lens ko cloudy bana sakta hai aur vision ko affect kar sakta hai.

🌿 Basic Eye Care

🧼

Hygiene

Eyes ko unnecessary rub karne se bachein aur personal hygiene ka dhyan rakhein.

☀️

Outdoor Safety

Bright sunlight aur outdoor conditions mein appropriate eye protection useful ho sakti hai.

📱

Screen Habits

Long screen sessions ke dauran breaks lena aur comfortable viewing distance maintain karna useful hai.

👓

Correct Glasses

Agar vision problem hai to proper eye examination ke baad recommended glasses use karein.

👦 Children ki Eyes

Bachchon mein vision problem education aur daily activities ko affect kar sakti hai. Agar child ko board dekhne mein difficulty, frequent squinting ya noticeable vision problem ho, to eye professional se assessment karana useful hai.

⭐ Simple Rule

Vision mein persistent change, blurry vision ya eye problem ko ignore na karein. Early assessment aur appropriate care kai conditions mein complications ko reduce karne mein help kar sakti hai.

⚠️ Kab jaldi medical help leni chahiye?

Serious eye symptoms ko ignore na karein

  • Suddenly vision kam ya change ho jana
  • Severe eye pain
  • Eye injury ya chemical exposure
  • Severe redness ke saath pain ya vision problem
  • Eye ke saamne unusual flashes/floaters ke saath sudden vision changes

👨‍⚕️ Eye Check-up

Regular eye assessment se vision problems ko identify karne mein help mil sakti hai. Glasses ki zarurat, eye disease ya persistent symptoms ke case mein qualified eye-care professional se examination karayein.
