Bukhaar & Common Illness

Bukhaar ke common reasons, ghar par basic care aur kab doctor ko dikhana chahiye — simple language mein.

Bukhaar kya hota hai?

Bukhaar body ka temperature normal se badh jana hai. Ye aksar infection ya kisi illness ki wajah se hota hai. Bukhaar khud koi disease nahi, balki body mein kisi problem ka ek sign ho sakta hai.

Common signs

🌡️

Body temperature badhna

Body ka temperature normal se zyada ho sakta hai.

🥶

Thand ya kampkapi

Bukhaar ke saath thand lagna ya kampkapi ho sakti hai.

😴

Weakness

Body mein weakness aur thakaan feel ho sakti hai.

🤕

Headache & body pain

Kuch illnesses mein headache aur body pain bhi ho sakta hai.

Bukhaar mein kya karein?

💧

Paani aur fluids

Paani aur suitable fluids lete rahein, taaki body mein paani ki kami na ho.

🛌

Proper rest

Body ko recover karne ke liye enough rest karein.

🍲

Halka khana

Agar bhookh ho to halka aur easily digest hone wala khana le sakte hain.

👀

Symptoms dekhein

Bukhaar ke saath aur kya symptoms aa rahe hain, un par bhi dhyan dein.

Bukhaar kaise theek hota hai?

Bukhaar ka treatment uske reason par depend karta hai. Bahut se common infections mein body rest, fluids aur time ke saath recover kar leti hai. Lekin agar symptoms badh rahe hain ya condition serious lag rahi hai, to doctor se check karwana zaroori hai.

Kab doctor ko dikhana chahiye?

⚠️ In signs ko ignore na karein

  • Saans lene mein dikkat
  • Seene mein dard
  • Bahut zyada weakness
  • Baar-baar vomiting
  • Bahut kam urine aana
  • Confusion ya unusual drowsiness
  • Condition continuously kharab hona

Important

Har bukhaar ka reason same nahi hota. Viral infection, flu, dengue aur doosri illnesses mein symptoms alag ho sakte hain. Isliye sirf bukhaar dekhkar khud se disease decide na karein.

Kya avoid karein?

🚫

Khud se disease decide na karein

Sirf symptoms dekhkar exact illness confirm nahi hoti.

💊

Khud se antibiotics na lein

Antibiotics har bukhaar ke liye useful nahi hoti. Doctor ki advice zaroori hai.

⏳

Condition ko ignore na karein

Agar symptoms serious ya continuously worse ho rahe hain, medical help lein.

Health information:

Ye page general health information ke liye hai. Serious symptoms ya worsening condition mein qualified healthcare professional se medical advice lena chahiye.
