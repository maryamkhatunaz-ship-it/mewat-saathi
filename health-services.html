Health Services

Health services ko samajhne, sahi jagah tak pahunchne aur available government health facilities ke baare mein basic information.

Important Health Services

Apni need ke according relevant service ko samjhein.

🚑

Emergency Help

Serious emergency mein delay na karein. Nearby appropriate emergency medical facility tak pahunchna important hai.

🏨

Hospital & PHC

Government health system mein primary level facilities aur hospitals basic se specialised care tak services de sakte hain.

🩺

Ayushman Arogya Mandir

Primary healthcare ke under preventive, general care, screening aur kai other health services available hain.

📱

Online Doctor

eSanjeevani government telemedicine service ke through eligible users remote medical consultation le sakte hain.

🧪

Tests & Diagnostics

Kuch health facilities par basic diagnostic aur screening services available ho sakti hain.

💉

Vaccination

Children, adolescents aur adults ke liye recommended vaccination services health system ke through mil sakti hain.

💳

Ayushman / Health Cover

Eligible families ke liye government health coverage schemes available hain. Eligibility check karna zaroori hai.

👩‍⚕️

ASHA / Health Worker

Community-level health workers basic health awareness, guidance aur healthcare services tak connection mein help karte hain.

🧠

Mental Health Help

Mental health concern ko ignore na karein. Professional support aur government tele-mental health services available hain.

📱 eSanjeevani

eSanjeevani India ki National Telemedicine Service hai. Iske through patients ko remote medical consultation ki facility mil sakti hai. Government sources ke according iska use patient-to-doctor aur healthcare-facility based telemedicine dono models mein hota hai.

Kaunsi Service Kab?

Simple way mein samjho.

🚑 Emergency

Agar situation serious ya rapidly worsening ho, online information par depend karne ke bajay emergency medical help lena important hai.

🏨 Hospital / PHC

Fever, infection, injury, recurring problem ya kisi health concern ke liye appropriate healthcare facility se assessment karwana better hai.

🩺 Ayushman Arogya Mandir

Government ke Ayushman Arogya Mandir network mein primary healthcare, screening, maternal-child health, oral, eye, mental health aur other services ka expanded range hai.

📱 Telemedicine

Jab in-person visit immediately possible na ho aur online consultation suitable ho, eSanjeevani jaise government telemedicine service ka option dekha ja sakta hai.

🧪 Tests

Diagnostic test ki need doctor ya healthcare professional situation ke according decide kar sakte hain. Har test har facility par available nahi hota.

💉 Vaccination

Vaccination ke liye nearby government health facility, vaccination centre ya healthcare worker se current availability aur schedule confirm karein.

💳 Health Coverage

Government health schemes mein eligibility aur covered services alag ho sakti hain. Official portal ya authorised health facility se details verify karein.

👩‍⚕️ Community Health Worker

ASHA aur other frontline health workers community ko health services, awareness aur appropriate facility tak pahunchne mein guide kar sakte hain.

🧠 Mental Health

Mental health concern ke liye professional help lena normal hai. Government ka Tele-MANAS programme bhi tele-mental health support provide karta hai.

⚠️ Important: Mewat Saathi health information ko simple banane ke liye hai. Ye doctor ki diagnosis ya emergency medical care ka replacement nahi hai. Health problem serious ho, symptoms worsen ho rahe hon, ya emergency lage, to qualified healthcare professional / appropriate medical facility se help lein.
