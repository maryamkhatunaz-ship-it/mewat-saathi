📘 JEE Main kya hai?

JEE Main India ka major national-level entrance examination hai jo undergraduate engineering aur architecture/planning admissions ke liye conduct kiya jata hai.

Engineering students ke liye sabse important route Paper 1 – B.E./B.Tech hai.

JEE Main ka result/NTA Score admission processes mein use hota hai aur JEE Advanced ke eligibility route se bhi iska important connection hai.

🎯 Marking Scheme

Section A — MCQ

  • Correct answer: +4
  • Incorrect answer: -1
  • Unanswered: 0

Section B — Numerical

  • Correct answer: +4
  • Incorrect answer: -1
  • Unanswered: 0

🧮 Marking ko example se samjho

Maan lo kisi subject mein tumne:


✅ 18 questions correct

❌ 4 questions wrong

⭕ 3 questions chhod diye

Score = (18 × 4) − (4 × 1) = 72 − 4 = 68 marks

⏱️ Exam Duration

JEE Main 2026 Paper 1 ke liye normal examination duration 3 hours hai.

Exact instructions aur special cases ke liye apne examination year ki official bulletin check karni chahiye.

💻 Exam Mode

Paper 1 computer-based test format mein conduct hota hai.

Isliye preparation mein sirf notebook questions nahi, balki timed computer-based mock tests bhi practice karna useful hai.

🎓 Eligibility

JEE Main ki eligibility har exam year ke official bulletin mein define hoti hai.

2026 bulletin ke according JEE Main 2026 ke liye candidates par age limit nahi thi.

2026 ke qualifying-examination rules ke according 2024 ya 2025 mein Class 12/equivalent pass karne wale aur 2026 mein appearing candidates eligible categories mein included the.

Lekin ek important difference hai: JEE Main mein appear karne ki eligibility aur kisi particular college/course ki admission eligibility same cheez zaroori nahi hai.

🔁 Sessions / Attempts

JEE Main 2026 ko two-session format mein conduct kiya gaya.

Candidate dono sessions mein appear kar sakta tha, aur multiple sessions mein appear karne par applicable NTA Score/ranking rules official bulletin ke according apply hote hain.

Future year ke exact session/attempt rules ke liye us year ki official NTA notification check karna zaroori hai.

🌐 Exam Language

JEE Main multiple Indian languages mein available hota hai, lekin available language options paper/session aur official notification ke according verify karni chahiye.

🔥 Student Motivation


JEE Main ko sirf “300 marks ka paper” mat samjho. Ye tumhare liye engineering ke multiple routes open karne wala examination ho sakta hai.


Lekin motivation ka matlab ye nahi ki har student ko IIT hi jaana hai. Engineering ke andar bhi bahut saare colleges, branches aur career paths hote hain.


Goal simple hai: apni preparation strong karo, exam ko samjho aur apne options maximum rakho.

📅 Preparation ka basic roadmap

1

NCERT / Basics

Class 11–12 concepts ko strong karo.

2

Chapter Practice

Har chapter ke questions systematically solve karo.

3

PYQs

Previous-year questions se actual exam pattern samjho.

4

Mock Tests

Full-length timed tests se speed aur accuracy improve karo.

5

Mistake Analysis

Sirf marks mat dekho. Galat questions ka reason identify karo.

  • Sirf theory padhna aur questions kam lagana

  • Mock test dena lekin analysis na karna
  • Negative marking ko ignore karna
  • Latest official notification check na karna
