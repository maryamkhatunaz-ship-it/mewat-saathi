🧭 Job Search Kaise Karein?

Bilkul simple — pehle qualification, phir job, phir official vacancy.

1

Qualification Select

8th, 10th, 12th, ITI, Diploma, Graduate, PG ya PhD choose karo.

2

Job Samjho

Job mein kya kaam hota hai aur basic requirements kya ho sakti hain, pehle samjho.

3

Location Search

Mewat/nearby ya India-wide location ke according vacancy search karo.

4

Official Apply

Vacancy ki exact eligibility check karke official portal par application karo.

🤝 Form Bharne Mein Help Chahiye?

Agar aap online form khud bhar sakte ho to official website par khud apply karo. Agar online process samajhne mein dikkat ho to authorised CSC ya applicable service centre se assistance li ja sakti hai.

💻 Khud Bhar Sakte Ho Official portal par directly registration aur application karo.
🏪 Help Ki Zaroorat Hai Authorised service centre se online process mein assistance lo.
🔐 OTP Safe Rakho OTP, password, PIN ya banking details kisi ko mat do. OTP ho to khud enter karo.

⚠️ Job Fraud Se Bachna

Kisi bhi job ko apply karne se pehle vacancy ki details aur official source verify karo.

  • Job ke naam par unnecessary payment mat karo.
  • Unknown WhatsApp/Telegram links par documents upload mat karo.
  • OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN aur password kisi ko mat do.
  • Company/recruiter ko verify karo.
  • Age, qualification aur last date khud check karo.
