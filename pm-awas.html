🏠 PM Awas Yojana

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana

PM Awas Yojana Government of India ki housing scheme hai, jiska aim eligible families ko affordable housing support provide karna hai.

Urban areas ke liye PMAY-U 2.0 ke under eligible families ko housing assistance ke different options milte hain.

PMAY-U 2.0 ke liye urban areas mein eligible EWS, LIG aur MIG households scheme ke rules ke according apply kar sakte hain.

Important condition ye hai ki applicant family ke paas India mein kahin bhi pucca house nahi hona chahiye, subject to scheme rules.

  • 🏠 Pucca house ownership check hoti hai
  • 💰 Household income dekhi ja sakti hai
  • 📍 Urban area eligibility important hai
  • 📄 Scheme ke documents aur verification required ho sakte hain

EWS: Annual household income up to ₹3 lakh.

LIG: Annual household income ₹3 lakh se ₹6 lakh tak.

MIG: Annual household income ₹6 lakh se ₹9 lakh tak under PMAY-U 2.0 guidelines.

PMAY-U 2.0 mein different components hain, jaise Beneficiary Led Construction, Affordable Housing in Partnership, Affordable Rental Housing aur Interest Subsidy Scheme.

Benefit applicant ki eligibility aur selected component ke according depend karta hai.

Exact documents applicant aur selected component ke according alag ho sakte hain.

  • 🪪 Aadhaar / Aadhaar Virtual ID
  • 📱 Mobile number
  • 💰 Income-related information
  • 🏠 Address-related information
  • 📄 Scheme ke according additional documents

Apply karne se pehle official portal par current document requirements verify karein.

PMAY-U 2.0 ke application process ke liye official government system aur concerned Urban Local Body (ULB) ke instructions follow karne chahiye.

Application submit karne se pehle eligibility aur documents verify karna important hai.

PMAY-U ki official website par scheme guidelines, application-related information aur latest updates mil sakte hain.
