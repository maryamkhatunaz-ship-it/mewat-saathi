🌾 PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

PM-KISAN Government of India ki Central Sector Scheme hai.

Is scheme ke under eligible landholding farmer families ko ₹6,000 per year ki financial support di jaati hai.

Ye amount 3 equal instalments mein directly beneficiary ke bank account mein transfer kiya jata hai.

Total benefit:

₹6,000 per year

Ye amount 3 equal instalments mein diya jata hai.

  • 💰 ₹2,000 — 1st instalment
  • 💰 ₹2,000 — 2nd instalment
  • 💰 ₹2,000 — 3rd instalment

Scheme eligible landholding farmer families ke liye hai. State/UT governments eligible farmer families ko scheme guidelines ke according identify karti hain.

Kuch categories scheme ke under excluded hain. Isliye final eligibility official PM-KISAN rules ke according check karni chahiye.

PM-KISAN registered farmers ke liye eKYC mandatory hai.

Official PM-KISAN portal par eKYC ke options available hain.

Farmer apni eKYC status ko official PM-KISAN system par check kar sakta hai.

New Farmer Registration official PM-KISAN portal par available hai.

Registration ke process mein Aadhaar authentication, mobile number aur state ki information required ho sakti hai.

Apply karne se pehle official portal par current requirements zaroor check karein.

PM-KISAN ke official portal par Know Your Status option available hai.

Wahan registration number ke through payment status aur account-related information check ki ja sakti hai.
