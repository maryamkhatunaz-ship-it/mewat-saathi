Vaccination

Vaccines kya hote hain, kyun zaroori hain aur vaccination ke time kin baaton ka dhyan rakhein.

Vaccine kya hoti hai?

Vaccine body ke immune system ko kuch specific infections se protection develop karne mein help karti hai. Vaccination diseases se bachav ka ek important way hai.

Vaccination kyun important hai?

🛡️

Protection

Vaccines kai serious infections se protection dene mein help karti hain.

👶

Children ke liye

Bachchon ke liye recommended vaccination schedule follow karna important hai.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Protection

Vaccination ka benefit individual ke saath community level par bhi ho sakta hai.

📅

Schedule

Vaccines ko recommended age aur schedule ke according lagwana chahiye.

Recommended Centre

Vaccination ke liye recognised healthcare facility ya recommended centre ka use karein.

Vaccine ke baad

Vaccine ke baad health worker ki instructions follow karein. Agar koi unusual ya serious reaction dikhe, to healthcare professional se medical advice lein.

⚠️ Kab medical help leni chahiye?

Serious symptoms ko ignore na karein

  • Saans lene mein dikkat
  • Behoshi ya severe weakness
  • Bahut unusual reaction
  • Condition rapidly worse hona

Ek important baat

Vaccination ko lekar social media ya kisi unknown source ki information par blindly trust na karein. Vaccine schedule aur personal health concerns ke liye qualified health professional ki advice follow karein.
