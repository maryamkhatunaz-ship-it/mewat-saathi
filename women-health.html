Women's Health

Women ki health, periods, pregnancy, nutrition, hygiene aur important warning signs ke baare mein simple information.

Women's Health kyun important hai?

Women ki health mein physical health ke saath reproductive health, nutrition, mental well-being aur regular health check-up bhi important hote hain. Kisi bhi unusual ya serious problem ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

Periods & Menstrual Health

🩸

Periods

Periods ek natural body process hai. Har person ka cycle thoda different ho sakta hai.

🧼

Hygiene

Periods ke time personal hygiene maintain karna aur sanitary products ko properly use karna important hai.

📅

Cycle par dhyan

Periods ki date aur unusual changes ko note karna useful ho sakta hai.

🩺

Unusual Changes

Bahut unusual bleeding, severe pain ya baar-baar unusual changes ho to healthcare professional se advice leni chahiye.

Pregnancy Care

🤰

Regular Check-up

Pregnancy ke time regular antenatal check-ups important hote hain.

🥗

Nutrition

Pregnancy mein balanced nutrition aur healthcare professional ki advice important hoti hai.

💊

Medicines

Pregnancy ke time koi medicine ya supplement khud se start na karein.

👩‍⚕️

Doctor ki Advice

Pregnancy mein unusual symptoms ya concern ho to healthcare professional se contact karein.

Nutrition & Daily Health

🥦

Balanced Food

Daily diet mein different nutritious foods ko include karna important hai.

💧

Paani

Din bhar enough fluids lena body ke liye important hai.

🛌

Sleep & Rest

Proper sleep aur rest overall health ko support karta hai.

🧘

Mental Well-being

Stress, anxiety ya emotional problems ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

⚠️ Kab Medical Help Len?

In signs ko ignore na karein

  • Bahut zyada ya unusual bleeding
  • Bahut severe pain
  • Pregnancy ke time unusual bleeding ya severe pain
  • Saans lene mein dikkat
  • Behoshi ya severe weakness
  • High fever ke saath condition worse hona
  • Koi symptom continuously badhna

Simple Rule

Apni body mein koi unusual change notice ho to usko ignore na karein. Problem serious lag rahi ho ya baar-baar ho rahi ho to qualified healthcare professional se advice lena better hai.

Kya Avoid Karein?

🚫

Problem Ignore na karein

Serious ya unusual symptoms ko unnecessarily delay na karein.

💊

Medicine khud se na lein

Especially pregnancy ke time medicines ke liye healthcare professional ki advice lein.

🔍

Self-diagnosis avoid karein

Internet par symptoms match karke khud se disease confirm na karein.

Health Information:
